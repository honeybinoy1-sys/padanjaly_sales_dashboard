# padanjaly_sales_dashboard
A data cleaning and sales analysis project built on the WooCommerce orders of Jagadha by Padanjaly, an Ayurvedic products business. The raw order export was cleaned in Power Query and analysed in a Power BI dashboard to understand revenue, products, payments, customers and seasonality.

# Project Overview
Item	Detail
Source	WooCommerce orders export
Size	690 orders (one row per order), 72 columns
Period	January 2023 to May 2026
Currency	Orders recorded in USD and INR; all amounts converted to INR

# Dashboard
The dashboard contains:
KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Guest Order %, Revenue in INR and Revenue in USD
Total Revenue and Total Orders by month
Total Revenue by payment method
Total Revenue by product
Total Orders by month

# Key Insights
Revenue: about ₹2.52M from 690 orders, roughly ₹3,650 per order.
Top product: Agnijith 90 gms brings in about half of revenue, and the wider Agnijith range around 80%.
Seasonality: orders peak in May and June and are lowest in September and October.
Busiest month: May 2026 had 83 orders, more than double any other month.
Highest revenue month: October 2025 (about ₹210k)
Payments: Razorpay accounts for 99.48% of revenue, cash on delivery for 0.52%.
International sales: USD orders (about $13K) make up close to 46% of revenue.
Guest checkout: 49.28% of orders are placed by guests

# Key results (Location Analysis)
Orders: 690 in total. India has 609 orders (88.26%) and international customers have 81 (11.74%). Customers are in 24 countries.
Top states by revenue: Kerala (about 0.36M), then Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu (about 0.17M each), Telangana (0.16M) and Karnataka (0.15M). Revenue drops sharply after the top five.
Lower-revenue states: Gujarat (0.06M), then Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, Delhi and Haryana (about 0.04M each).
Top cities by orders: Bengaluru (57), Hyderabad (33), Thiruvananthapuram (28), Thrissur (27) and Chennai (25).
Billing vs shipping: 98.84% of orders (682) have matching billing and shipping cities.
Customer loyalty: 6.50% of registered customers placed repeat orders

# Customer Analysis Dashboard 

# Project Overview
This project analyses customer purchase behaviour using Power BI to understand customer types, order patterns, product spending, and monthly order trends. The dashboard helps identify repeat purchasing patterns and potential customer retention opportunities using dataset-based insights.

# Objectives
Analyse registered customers and guest orders.
Compare registered orders with guest orders.
Identify one-time and repeat registered customers.
Analyse product-wise spending.
Track monthly and yearly order trends.

# Dashboard KPIs
Registered Customers: 323
Repeat Registered Customers: 21
Guest Order Percentage: 49.28%

# Key Insights
The dataset contains 323 registered customers: 302 one-time customers and 21 repeat customers.
Registered orders account for 350 orders (50.72%), while guest orders account for 340 orders (49.28%).
Kerala recorded the highest order volume among the displayed states, with 235 orders.
Agnijith 90 gms is the highest-spending product shown, at approximately 1.24M in the dashboard's displayed units.
May 2026 recorded the highest monthly order volume in the displayed period, with 83 orders.
